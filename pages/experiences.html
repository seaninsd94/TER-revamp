The Complete Experience

From Vision to Celebration

Tasteful Studio offers more than curated collections—we offer a complete luxury event experience. Our premium services are designed for clients who want every detail handled with the same level of care and sophistication as the event itself.

Event Planning & Coordination

Our experienced planners work alongside you from concept to completion. We manage timelines, vendor coordination, and day-of logistics so you can be fully present for your celebration.

Personal Concierge Service

A dedicated concierge assigned to your event, serving as your single point of contact from the first consultation through final breakdown. Available for our Estate Affair and Grand Celebration packages.

Custom Event Design

Our design team creates bespoke floor plans, curated color palettes, and styling concepts tailored to your vision. We transform spaces into immersive environments that reflect your personal aesthetic.

White-Glove Delivery & Styling

Our professional installation team delivers, assembles, and styles every element with meticulous attention to detail. We arrive early and stay until every piece is positioned to perfection.

Curated Lounge Vignettes

Designed lounging environments featuring plush settees, sculptural accent chairs, artisan side tables, and statement cocktail tables—creating intimate gathering spaces within your event.

Ambient Lighting Design

From crystal chandeliers and bistro string lights to dramatic uplighting—our lighting designers craft the perfect atmosphere to complement your venue and elevate the mood throughout the evening.
