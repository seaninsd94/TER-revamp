White-Glove Event Support

Event Concierge Services

We don't just deliver rentals—we manage how your event feels.

Our Event Concierge service ensures every space functions beautifully for your guests, from the first arrival to the final transition. Working alongside your planner, we oversee layout refinement, guest flow, and on-site adjustments so your event feels effortless, intentional, and elevated.

This service is designed for clients who value comfort, atmosphere, and seamless execution—not just items on a floor plan.

Before the Event

  • ✓ Collaborates with your planner on layout intent
  • ✓ Reviews guest count, venue constraints, and transitions
  • ✓ Pre-plans lighting zones, lounge placement, and flow

During the Event

  • ✓ Oversees environment execution on-site
  • ✓ Makes real-time adjustments to furniture and lighting
  • ✓ Coordinates quietly with catering, bar, and venue teams
  • ✓ Ensures cocktail hour, reception, and transitions feel natural

After Transitions

  • ✓ Manages environment shifts without disrupting guests
  • ✓ Protects the reveal and pacing of the event
  • ✓ Maintains visual cohesion throughout the evening

Who Concierge Is For

  • ● Weddings with 150+ guests
  • ● Multi-space events (ceremony, cocktail, reception)
  • ● Tented or estate weddings
  • ● Corporate and executive events
  • ● Clients investing in full environments rather than individual rentals

How Concierge Works With Your Planner

Your planner remains the creative and logistical lead.

Our concierge team supports their vision by managing the physical environment and guest experience in real time.

Think of it as an added layer of hospitality—not an extra vendor to manage.
