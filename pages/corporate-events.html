Common Questions

Corporate Event FAQ

What makes Tasteful Studio different from other corporate event rental companies?

We provide the most comprehensive, stress-free corporate event production in Southern California. Unlike traditional rental companies where you coordinate multiple vendors, we handle everything: custom tent structures, executive furnishings, production lighting, climate control, power distribution, staging, and professional installation and breakdown. You get a dedicated account manager and complete peace of mind.

What areas do you serve for corporate events?

We serve all of Southern California including San Diego, Orange County (Newport Beach, Irvine, Laguna Beach), Los Angeles (Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Downtown LA), Santa Barbara, and Palm Springs. Our corporate event production team regularly works at convention centers, corporate campuses, hotels, resorts, and private venues throughout the region.

Do you provide insurance and compliance documentation?

Yes, we are fully insured with $2M+ in liability coverage and provide Certificates of Insurance (COI) upon request. We meet the requirements of major corporate venues, hotels, convention centers, and private estates throughout Southern California. Net-30 payment terms are available with detailed invoicing for procurement teams.

What types of corporate events do you produce?

We produce corporate galas and awards ceremonies, brand activations and product launches, executive retreats and offsites, corporate holiday parties, investor and stakeholder events, fundraising galas, conferences, trade shows, company milestone celebrations, and team appreciation events. Each event receives custom scope proposals tailored to your objectives.

How far in advance should we book corporate event production?

For large-scale corporate galas and tented events, we recommend booking 3-6 months in advance to ensure availability of premium tent structures and production equipment. Executive retreats and smaller corporate events can often be accommodated with 4-8 weeks notice, depending on scope and season.
