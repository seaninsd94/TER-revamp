Orange County Corporate Event FAQ

What corporate event venues do you serve in Orange County?

We produce corporate events throughout Orange County including Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, Irvine, Dana Point, Huntington Beach, and Costa Mesa. We have established relationships with venues like Montage Laguna Beach, Pelican Hill, Ritz-Carlton Laguna Niguel, and numerous private estates and corporate campuses.

What makes your corporate event design different?

We are a full-service corporate event design firm, not a rental company. We create commissioned environments that integrate brand storytelling, experiential design, and white-glove production. Each event receives custom scope proposals tailored to your organization's objectives and stakeholder expectations.

Do you produce tented corporate events in Orange County?

Yes, tented corporate events are a specialty. We install climate-controlled clearspan and sailcloth structures at private estates, country clubs, corporate campuses, and coastal venues throughout Orange County. Our tented environments include production lighting, staging, and executive lounge zones.
