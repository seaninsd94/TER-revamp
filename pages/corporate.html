Common Questions

Corporate Event Production FAQ

What is included in corporate event production?

Our corporate event production includes full-service environment design: custom tent structures with climate control, executive seating and furnishings, production lighting, staging, power distribution, VIP lounge zones, and professional installation and teardown. We also provide dedicated account management and concierge-level coordination throughout your event.

What areas do you serve for corporate events?

We serve all of Southern California including San Diego, Orange County, Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, and Palm Springs. Our corporate event production team regularly works at venues in La Jolla, Rancho Santa Fe, Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, Beverly Hills, and surrounding areas.

How far in advance should we book corporate event production?

For large-scale corporate galas and tented events, we recommend booking 3-6 months in advance to ensure availability of premium tent structures and production equipment. Executive retreats and smaller corporate events can often be accommodated with 4-8 weeks notice, depending on scope and season.

Do you provide insurance and compliance documentation?

Yes, we are fully insured and provide Certificates of Insurance (COI) upon request. We meet the requirements of major corporate venues, hotels, and private estates throughout Southern California. Net-30 payment terms are available with detailed invoicing for procurement teams.

What types of corporate events do you produce?

We produce corporate galas and awards ceremonies, brand activations and product launches, executive retreats and offsites, corporate holiday parties, investor and stakeholder events, fundraising galas, conferences, and company milestone celebrations. Each event receives custom scope proposals tailored to your objectives.
