Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book? +

We recommend booking 9-12 months in advance for peak season dates (April-October). For off-season weddings, 6-8 months is typically sufficient. Popular estate venues in Rancho Santa Fe and La Jolla book quickly, so earlier is always better. We can sometimes accommodate shorter timelines depending on availability.

Do you work with our existing vendors? +

Absolutely. We collaborate seamlessly with wedding planners, florists, caterers, and photographers you've already chosen. We also have trusted vendor relationships throughout San Diego County and can provide recommendations if needed. Our goal is to enhance your team, not replace it.

What's your pricing range? +

Our wedding environments typically range from $8,000 for intimate celebrations (under 50 guests) to $50,000+ for grand estate productions. Most San Diego weddings fall in the $15,000-$30,000 range. Every wedding is custom-designed, so we'll provide a detailed proposal after understanding your vision, venue, and guest count during our discovery call.

What venues do you work with in San Diego? +

We specialize in private estates and non-traditional venues throughout San Diego County, including properties in Rancho Santa Fe, La Jolla, Del Mar, Coronado, Fairbanks Ranch, and Solana Beach. We're experienced with the unique requirements of estate weddings, including power, permitting, and site logistics.

What's included in your service? +

Our full-service wedding environments include design consultation, site visits, detailed renderings, all rental equipment (tents, furniture, lighting, decor), professional installation and teardown, and on-site management on your wedding day. We handle every detail so you can focus on celebrating.
