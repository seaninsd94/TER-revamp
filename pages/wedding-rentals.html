Common Questions

Wedding Rental FAQ

What makes Tasteful Studio different from other wedding rental companies?

We provide the most comprehensive, stress-free wedding rental experience in Southern California. Unlike traditional rental companies where you coordinate multiple vendors, we handle everything: custom tent structures, luxury furnishings, designer lighting, climate control, power distribution, and white-glove setup and breakdown. You get a single point of contact and complete peace of mind.

What areas do you serve for wedding rentals?

We serve all of Southern California including San Diego, La Jolla, Rancho Santa Fe, Del Mar, Coronado, Carlsbad, Temecula, Orange County (Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, Dana Point), Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, and Palm Springs. We regularly work at estates, vineyards, resorts, and private venues throughout the region.

How far in advance should I book wedding rentals?

For peak wedding season (April-October), we recommend booking 6-12 months in advance to ensure availability of our premium tent structures and furnishings. Off-season weddings can often be accommodated with 3-6 months notice. Contact us early to secure your preferred date.

What is included in your wedding rental packages?

Our comprehensive packages include luxury tent structures with climate control, designer lighting and chandeliers, premium tables and seating, elegant linens and napkins, dance floors, power distribution, professional installation and breakdown, and dedicated concierge service. We also offer cocktail hour setups, lounge furniture, and infrastructure for estate and remote venues.

Do you work with wedding planners and coordinators?

Absolutely! We regularly collaborate with San Diego and Southern California's top wedding planners and venue coordinators. Our team integrates seamlessly with your planning team, providing detailed floor plans, vendor coordination, and timeline management to ensure flawless execution.
