Newport Beach Corporate Gala FAQ

What Newport Beach venues do you work with for corporate galas?

We produce corporate galas at Newport Beach's premier venues including Balboa Bay Resort, Newport Beach Country Club, Fashion Island, and private waterfront estates. We also work at venues in Corona del Mar and Balboa Island for exclusive corporate events.

What size corporate galas can you produce in Newport Beach?

We produce corporate galas ranging from intimate executive gatherings of 50 guests to large-scale tented events for 500+ attendees. Our clearspan tent structures can accommodate grand galas while maintaining the sophisticated aesthetic Newport Beach organizations expect.

Do you provide production for nonprofit fundraising galas in Newport Beach?

Yes, we regularly produce fundraising galas for Newport Beach's philanthropic community. Our full-scale production includes auction staging, sponsor recognition displays, VIP donor experiences, and environments designed to inspire generosity and maximize fundraising outcomes.
