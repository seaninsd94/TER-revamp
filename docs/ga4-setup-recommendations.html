Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Setup Guide

Tasteful Studio | April 2026

Current Status: GA4 is already installed on your site (Property ID: G-ZC8TN9VZC9). This guide covers configuration and optimization to get actionable insights from your data.

Why GA4 Matters for Your Business

As a luxury event design company with long booking cycles (6-18 months), you need to understand:

  • Which channels drive your best leads - Google Ads vs organic vs referrals
  • Which pages convert visitors to inquiries - so you can improve underperformers
  • How users navigate your site - to optimize the journey to contact
  • What content resonates - to inform your content strategy

Immediate Setup Actions

Critical

1. Configure Conversion Events

You need to track when visitors complete important actions. Here's what to set up:

Event Name Trigger Value
form_submission Contact form submitted High (Primary Conversion)
corporate_form_submission Corporate form submitted High (Primary Conversion)
phone_click Click on phone number Medium (Secondary Conversion)
email_click Click on email link Medium (Secondary Conversion)

How to set up in GA4:

  1. Go to GA4 > Admin > Events
  2. Click "Create event"
  3. Configure based on existing events (form_submit, click events)
  4. Go to Admin > Conversions and mark the events as conversions
Note: Your contact form already sends a Santa_Form_Submissions event. Make sure this is marked as a conversion in GA4 Admin > Conversions.
Critical

2. Link Google Ads to GA4

Since you're running Google Ads, linking accounts is essential for:

  • Seeing which ads drive form submissions
  • Creating remarketing audiences
  • Importing conversions back to Google Ads

Steps:

  1. In GA4, go to Admin > Product Links > Google Ads Links
  2. Click "Link" and select your Google Ads account
  3. Enable "Auto-tagging" in Google Ads if not already on
  4. In Google Ads, import GA4 conversions: Tools > Conversions > New > Import from GA4
High Priority

3. Set Up Enhanced Measurement

GA4 has built-in tracking for common actions. Verify these are enabled:

  1. Go to Admin > Data Streams > [Your Web Stream]
  2. Click "Enhanced measurement"
  3. Ensure these are ON:
    • Page views
    • Scrolls (reaches 90% depth)
    • Outbound clicks
    • Site search (if applicable)
    • Form interactions
    • Video engagement (if you add video testimonials)
High Priority

4. Create Key Audiences

Build audiences for remarketing and analysis:

Audience Definition Use Case
High-Intent Visitors Visited contact OR packages page Remarketing ads
Form Starters Started but didn't submit form Abandoned form follow-up
Corporate Prospects Visited corporate pages B2B remarketing
Wedding Prospects Visited wedding/packages pages Wedding remarketing
Engaged Users 3+ pages, 2+ min on site Lookalike audiences

To create: Go to Admin > Audiences > New Audience

Medium Priority

5. Custom Dimensions

Track additional context about your visitors:

Dimension Scope Example Values
event_type_interest Event wedding, corporate, birthday
package_interest Event elegant-reception, estate-affair
how_heard User referral, google, social-media
landing_page_type Session homepage, location-page, service-page

Key Reports to Monitor

Weekly Check (5 minutes)

  • Realtime: Verify tracking is working
  • Acquisition Overview: Where are visitors coming from?
  • Conversions: How many form submissions this week?

Monthly Analysis (30 minutes)

  • Traffic Acquisition: Compare channels month-over-month
  • Pages and Screens: Which pages get the most views?
  • Conversion Paths: What pages do converters visit before submitting?
  • Landing Page Performance: Which pages bring in converting traffic?
  • Tech > Browser/Device: Are mobile users converting?

Quarterly Deep Dive (1-2 hours)

  • Compare conversion rates by traffic source
  • Identify high-value pages (time on page, scroll depth)
  • Analyze form abandonment
  • Review Google Ads performance in GA4
  • Adjust remarketing audiences

Google Ads Integration

Since you're running ads to your landing pages, here's how to optimize:

1. Import Conversions to Google Ads

  1. In Google Ads: Tools & Settings > Conversions
  2. Click "+ New conversion action" > Import > Google Analytics 4
  3. Select your form_submission and phone_click events
  4. Set appropriate conversion windows (30-90 days for luxury)

2. Create Remarketing Campaigns

Target visitors who didn't convert:

  • Visited contact page but didn't submit
  • Spent 60+ seconds on packages page
  • Viewed 3+ pages

Show them display ads reminding them to inquire.

3. Analyze Landing Page Performance

For your current landing pages:

  • /pages/rancho-santa-fe-luxury-event-design.html
  • /pages/san-diego-luxury-wedding-design.html

Check in GA4: Reports > Engagement > Landing page. Compare:

  • Bounce rate (lower is better)
  • Avg. engagement time (higher is better)
  • Conversions (form submissions)

Search Console Integration

You mentioned you have Search Console access. Here's how to use that data:

Connect to GA4

  1. In GA4: Admin > Product Links > Search Console Links
  2. Click "Link" and select your Search Console property
  3. New reports will appear under Acquisition > Search Console
