Outreach Schedule

Week Actions
Week 1 Create profiles on The Knot and WeddingWire. Join ILEA and WIPA San Diego.
Week 2 Submit first real wedding to Style Me Pretty. Apply to Junebug Vendors directory.
Week 3 Contact San Diego Style Weddings and California Wedding Day with submissions.
Week 4 Reach out to 5 local wedding planners for introductory meetings.
Week 5-8 Submit to Martha Stewart Weddings, Brides. Pitch to San Diego Magazine.
Ongoing Attend 2+ industry events per month. Submit 1 wedding per month to publications.

Tracking Your Progress

Create a spreadsheet to track:

  • Publication name and contact
  • Date of outreach
  • Status (Submitted / Under Review / Published / Declined)
  • Link to published feature (for "As Featured In" badges)
  • Follow-up dates

Once You Get Featured

When a publication features your work:

  1. Add their logo to your "As Featured In" section on the homepage
  2. Share on social media and tag the publication
  3. Send a thank-you to the editor
  4. Link to the feature from your Stories/Portfolio page
  5. Update your vendor profiles with the press mention
