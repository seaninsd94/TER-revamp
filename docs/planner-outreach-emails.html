Best Practices for Planner Outreach

  • Research first. Reference a specific event they've done or their design aesthetic.
  • Personalize the opening. Replace generic greetings with something specific: "I loved the garden reception you designed for the Martinez wedding."
  • Keep emails under 150 words. Planners are busy. Respect their time.
  • Never attach files in initial emails. Offer to send materials if they're interested.
  • Follow up thoughtfully. If no response after 3 emails, wait 30 days before re-engaging.
  • Track everything. Note which planners open, respond, or request materials.
  • Be patient. Partnerships develop over time. One event referral can lead to many more.

Subject Line Alternatives

  • Email 1: "Story-Driven Environments for Your Clients" / "A Different Approach to Event Design"
  • Email 2: "How We Turned a Memory Into an Environment" / "A Recent Collaboration Example"
  • Email 3: "Partnership Opportunity" / "Resources for Your Upcoming Events"
