Equipment Checklist

Standard Venue Setup

  • Triple spider boxes (twist-lock distribution)
  • Extension cords (10x 50ft white)
  • High peak tent fans (6x ceiling mount)
  • Multi-zone power distribution (4 zones)
  • Dedicated stage power circuit
  • Cable covers and management system (3 sets)
  • Stage/presentation flooring

Outdoor/Remote Venue Setup

  • 60-75kW towable diesel generator (primary)
  • 20kW backup generator (standby)
  • 24 Ton AC unit with heater
  • 36ft+ HVAC ducting system
  • Subflooring (6,000 sq ft)
  • 6-stall luxury restroom trailer
  • Generator fuel (50-60 gal/day)
  • Multi-zone power distribution (3 spider boxes)
  • Dedicated stage power circuit
  • Comprehensive pathway lighting system
Important: Grand Gala events require advance coordination with our production team. For events with live entertainment, heavy AV production, or broadcast requirements, additional power and backup systems may be necessary. Please schedule a site survey at least 30 days prior to your event.
Note: Climate requirements calculated at 1 ton AC per 100-150 sq ft. Generator sizing includes headroom for lighting, AV equipment, and climate control startup loads. A backup generator is strongly recommended for events of this scale to ensure uninterrupted service.
