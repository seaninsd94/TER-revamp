Equipment Checklist

Standard Venue

  • Spider box distribution (3x)
  • Extension cords (10x 50ft)
  • Dedicated stage power circuit
  • Tent subflooring (6,000 sf)
  • Finished floor surface
  • High peak tent fans (6x)
  • Portable AC units (as needed)
  • Cable covers (3 sets)
  • Venue restroom access confirmed

Outdoor/Remote Venue

  • 60-75kW diesel generator (primary)
  • 20kW backup generator
  • Generator power cords (4x 75ft)
  • Spider box distribution (3x)
  • Tent subflooring (6,000 sf)
  • Finished floor surface
  • 24 Ton AC/heater unit
  • HVAC ducting (36ft+)
  • Circulation fans (6x)
  • 6-stall restroom trailer
  • Pathway lighting system
  • Generator fuel (50-60 gal/day)
Important: Grand Gala events require advance coordination with our production team. For live entertainment, heavy AV, or broadcast requirements, additional power and backup systems may be necessary. Schedule a site survey at least 30 days prior.
Note: Climate requirements based on 1 ton AC per 100-150 sq ft. A backup generator is strongly recommended for events of this scale to ensure uninterrupted service.
