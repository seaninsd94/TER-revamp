Equipment Checklist

Standard Venue Setup

  • Dual spider boxes (twist-lock distribution)

  • Multi-zone power distribution
  • Cable covers and management system (2 sets)

Outdoor/Remote Venue Setup

  • 40kW towable diesel generator
  • 12 Ton AC unit with heater
  • 24ft HVAC ducting (2x 12ft sections)

  • Dual spider boxes + distribution

  • Backup fan units
Note: Climate requirements calculated at 1 ton AC per 100-150 sq ft. Generator sizing includes headroom for lighting, AV equipment, and climate control startup loads. Actual requirements may vary based on venue conditions, season, and specific event needs. For events with heavy AV production, additional power capacity may be required.
