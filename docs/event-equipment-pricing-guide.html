Package Cost Summary

Scenario A: Full Outdoor (Private Estate, No Infrastructure)

Typical Venues: Private estates in Rancho Santa Fe, vineyard fields, beach locations, open ranch properties, backyard weddings on large properties
Power: Generator, spider box, quad boxes, extension cords, cable ramps
Flooring: Full subflooring, finished flooring throughout, dance floor, ADA ramps
Sanitation: Elegant waste bins, recycling, luxury restroom trailer
Climate: Tent sidewalls, patio heaters OR fans, full climate support

Scenario B: Semi-Outdoor (Winery, Garden Venue with Power)

Typical Venues: Bernardo Winery, Twin Oaks Garden Estate, Cordiano Winery, Ponte Winery, estate venues with existing patios, covered pavilion areas
Power: Distribution only (spider box, quad boxes, cords) – no generator needed
Flooring: Dance floor + transition elements only – venue has prepared surface
Sanitation: Waste bins and recycling only – venue has adequate restrooms
Climate: Sidewalls, heaters or fans (reduced quantity) – partially protected
